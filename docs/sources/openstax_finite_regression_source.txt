ALIASES: least-squares slope | slope from centered sums
EXPRESSION: covariance_sum / x_variance_sum
INPUTS: covariance_sum, x_variance_sum
ASSUMPTIONS: centered cross-product and centered x sum of squares are declared; x variation is nonzero
CONSTRAINTS: not_equal_integer:x_variance_sum=0
