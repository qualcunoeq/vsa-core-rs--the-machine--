EVIDENCE: A finite transition matrix has a declared state ordering and normalized rows; a stationary distribution is a probability vector preserved by the transition operator.
SCOPE: This implementation adds only exact rational row-stochastic matrices with at most four states and an exact rank/zero-residual certificate.
BOUNDARY: Continuous-time chains, approximate numerics, undisclosed row/column conventions, larger systems, and non-unique stationary sets remain unsupported.
