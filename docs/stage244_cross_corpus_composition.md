# Stage 244 — cross-corpus composition

Two independently discovered source corpora were exposed to one route-blind
frontend: the original economics/statistics/complex corpus and the fresh
geometry/health/unit/interpolation corpus.

Results:

* corpus A: 6 modules / 21 records;
* corpus B: 12 modules / 15 records;
* 360 exact gap observations across 18 clusters and 18 proposals;
* 3 utility-selected modules from each corpus, six combined, at utility 400
  and cost 20;
* 600 source cases authorized exactly once;
* 100 ambiguous/unknown boundary cases refused;
* 700/700 total decisions exact;
* 4,200/4,200 frontend replays and tamper rejections;
* zero route leakage, false authorizations, false denials, or live mutations;
* curriculum manifest unchanged.

The combined route preserved source-catalog identity: a valid formula from one
corpus could not be authorized by an unrelated catalog, and ambiguous target
text remained refused across the merged set.
