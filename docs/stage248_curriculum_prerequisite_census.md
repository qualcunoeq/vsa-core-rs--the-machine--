# Stage 248 — broad curriculum prerequisite census

This campaign tests prerequisite discovery across a mixed artifact corpus,
rather than one feature-specific route. Known typed artifacts are repeated
across eight bounded curriculum gates; 120 unknown artifacts are deliberately
kept outside the manifest. Discovery and learning plans remain shadow-only.

Results:

* 1,080 cases: 960 known and 120 unknown;
* 960/960 known artifacts resolved to complete prerequisite closures;
* 120/120 unknown artifacts refused as unowned;
* 1,080/1,080 observation replays and tamper rejections;
* 960 bounded gap proposals replayed and tamper-rejected, with all 120 unknown
  failure gates refused;
* exact clustering produced eight repeated typed-artifact clusters and 120
  one-case residual clusters, without broad-label merging;
* nine learning plans were produced: eight promotable candidates and one
  candidate blocked by an unknown prerequisite;
* all nine plans replayed and tamper-rejected;
* 16 dependency-edge checks ran, with eight cyclic proposals rejected;
* the parent manifest remained unchanged, with zero false authorizations,
  false denials, or live mutations.
