# Stage A: bounded polynomial algebra

This curriculum pack adds exact polynomial operations over an explicitly
declared small prime field: addition, multiplication, Euclidean division,
monic gcd, evaluation, exhaustive bounded roots, and exact quadratic factors.
It refuses non-prime or implicit coefficient domains, degree above eight,
minimal-polynomial claims without a linear-map witness, and unbounded
factorization.

The independent campaign contains 240 cases: 120 supported, 40 ambiguous, and
80 refused. Every result is replay-verified and tampered receipts are rejected.
