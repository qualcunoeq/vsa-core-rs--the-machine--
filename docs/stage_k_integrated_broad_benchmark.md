# Stage K: integrated broad benchmark

This checkpoint exercises four independently validated bounded curriculum
routes—combinatorics, elementary number theory, calculus, and scalar ODEs—over
5,000 deterministic cases. Six tenths are supported, two tenths deliberately
ambiguous, and two tenths outside the declared domains. Every route records
its expected terminal outcome, replay receipt, tamper result, and first-gate
localization. The benchmark does not mutate curriculum or production state.

The generated report is `docs/stage_k_integrated_broad_benchmark.json`.
