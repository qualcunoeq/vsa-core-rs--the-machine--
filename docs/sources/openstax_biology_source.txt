Governed claims used by the bounded pack:
- DNA sequences use the bases adenine (A), thymine (T), cytosine (C), and guanine (G).
- Complementary pairing is A-T and G-C.
- DNA strands have orientation; reverse-complement output is distinct from an aligned complement.

The pack does not infer RNA transcription, codon translation, mutation effects,
gene identity, or phenotype from these claims.
