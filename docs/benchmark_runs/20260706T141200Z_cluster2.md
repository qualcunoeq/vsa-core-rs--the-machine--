# Cognition benchmark cluster 20260706T141200Z_cluster2

Documented: 2026-07-06T14:12:37Z

## Purpose

This second cluster targets the weak points found in the previous run: memory-pressure latency, adaptation stability, and the failure of large/max adaptation runs to produce JSONL output.

## Launched Workloads

- 40 `adaptation --scale medium` jobs across seeds `40000..40039`, capped at 75 minutes each.
- 32 `memory-pressure --scale medium` jobs across seeds `41000..41031`, capped at 2 hours each.
- 6 `memory-pressure --scale large` jobs across seeds `42000..42005`, capped at 3 hours each.
- 2 `adaptation --scale large` probe jobs across seeds `43000..43001`, capped at 4 hours each.
- 8 `qa-depth --scale max` jobs across seeds `44000..44007`, capped at 2 hours each.

## Expected Signal

- Medium adaptation should validate whether the zero-regression result holds across many more seeds.
- Medium and large memory-pressure should quantify the retrieval latency curve under heavy concurrent pressure.
- Large adaptation probes should determine whether the earlier missing JSONL outputs were time-cap failures or actual hangs.
- QA depth under saturation should show whether deep reasoning latency gets worse when memory/adaptation jobs are competing for CPU.
